Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 19, 2026

1. Data We Collect

When you use Spendly, we collect the information you provide directly, such as your name, email address, and password when you register, along with the expense records, categories, amounts, and dates you log while using the Service.

2. How We Use Your Data

We use your data to provide core functionality — logging expenses, generating spending summaries, and maintaining your account. We may also use aggregated, de-identified data to improve the Service. We do not use your personal expense data for advertising purposes.

3. Data Storage

Your account and expense data are stored securely and retained for as long as your account remains active. You may request deletion of your account and all associated data at any time, after which your data will be permanently removed from our systems.

4. Third Party Services

Spendly does not sell or share your personal data with third parties for marketing purposes. We may rely on trusted third-party infrastructure providers (such as hosting and email delivery) solely to operate the Service, and these providers are bound to handle your data securely.

5. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy or how your data is handled, please reach out to us through the contact details provided on your account page.
